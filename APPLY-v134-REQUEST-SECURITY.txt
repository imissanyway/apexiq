PROTECTIONS
- Mandatory server-side Turnstile validation.
- Turnstile hostname and action validation.
- 4 requests/hour per hashed IP.
- 3 requests/6 hours per hashed browser/device.
- 2 requests/day per hashed Apex username.
- Raw IP addresses are never stored.
- Block & delete blocks available username/IP/device identifiers for 180 days.
- Deleted/blocked requests are retained as sanitized audit records for 30 days.
- Existing push notifications fire only after the request passes security.
